## What is Role Reactor Bot?

Role Reactor Bot is an all-in-one Discord community engine and role management system. Beyond interactive emoji role reactions, it powers support tickets, starboards, community giveaways, moderation auto-escalation, voice channel permissions, and credit-based AI avatar tools.

**Core Features:**

- **Self-Assignable Roles** - Click emoji reactions to assign or remove roles automatically (`/role-reactions`)
- **Starboard System** - Highlight community-starred messages with dynamic heat-map embeds (`/starboard`)
- **Giveaway System** - Host automated giveaways with role/booster weighted entries and claim timers (`/giveaway`)
- **Ticket Management System** - Custom support ticket panels, role assignments, and HTML transcripts (`/ticket`)
- **Moderation & Warning System** - Auto-escalation, warning history, bulk timeouts, bans, kicks, and purges (`/moderation`)
- **Voice Control Roles** - Auto-disconnect, mute, deafen, or move members based on configured voice roles (`/voice-roles`)
- **Temporary & Scheduled Roles** - Auto-expiring access and natural language time scheduling (`/temp-roles`, `/schedule-role`)
- **Welcome & Goodbye Systems** - Customizable welcome and goodbye messages with member placeholders (`/welcome`, `/goodbye`)
- **Web Dashboard Image Tools** - Web-based image resizing, compression, format conversion, and AI upscaling
- **Core Credit System** - Credit-based economy for AI avatar generation and premium dashboard image tools (`/core balance`)
- **XP & Leaderboards** - High-performance level tracking, custom rank titles, and leaderboards (`/level`, `/leaderboard`)
- **Community Games & Polls** - Interactive minigames including Would You Rather (`/wyr`), Rock Paper Scissors (`/rps`), 8ball (`/8ball`), and native polls (`/poll`)

## Available Commands

### General Commands (All Users)

- [`/core balance`](/docs/commands/general/core) - View your Core balance and tier status
- [`/vote`](/docs/commands/general/vote) - Vote on top.gg to earn free daily Cores
- [`/level`](/docs/commands/general/level) - View your rank title, level, and XP progress
- [`/leaderboard`](/docs/commands/general/leaderboard) - Browse server member leaderboards
- [`/wyr`](/docs/commands/general/wyr) - Play interactive Would You Rather questions
- [`/rps`](/docs/commands/general/rps) - Challenge members to Rock Paper Scissors
- [`/avatar`](/docs/commands/general/avatar) - Generate custom AI avatars
- [`/poll create`](/docs/commands/general/poll) - Create interactive Discord polls
- [`/userinfo`](/docs/commands/general/userinfo) - View detailed Discord member profiles & moderation history
- [`/serverinfo`](/docs/commands/general/serverinfo) - View server statistics, boost level, and Pro Engine status
- [`/help`](/docs/commands/general/help) - Interactive command guide with autocomplete
- [`/ping`](/docs/commands/general/ping) - Check bot API latency and system status

### Admin Commands (Administrators)

- [`/starboard setup`](/docs/commands/admin/starboard) - Configure starboard channel, emoji, and reaction thresholds
- [`/giveaway`](/docs/commands/admin/giveaway) - Create, edit, list, end, or reroll giveaways
- [`/ticket`](/docs/commands/admin/ticket) - Setup ticket panels, manage tickets, and export transcripts
- [`/role-bundle`](/docs/commands/admin/role-bundle) - Create reusable role groups for reaction role setups
- [`/role-reactions`](/docs/commands/admin/role-reactions) - Create and manage self-assignable role selection panels
- [`/moderation`](/docs/commands/admin/moderation) - Perform bulk warnings, timeouts, bans, kicks, and purges
- [`/voice-roles`](/docs/commands/admin/voice-roles) - Configure automated voice channel role rules
- [`/temp-roles`](/docs/commands/admin/temp-roles) - Assign temporary roles with bulk role targeting
- [`/schedule-role`](/docs/commands/admin/schedule-role) - Schedule one-time or recurring role assignments
- [`/welcome setup`](/docs/commands/admin/welcome) - Configure welcome channel, embeds, and auto-roles
- [`/goodbye setup`](/docs/commands/admin/goodbye) - Configure leave messages and goodbye embeds
- [`/xp settings`](/docs/commands/admin/xp) - Toggle experience system and level-up announcements
